Stage 2: guarded distant coast integrated into the ordinary Fleet runtime

FleetOptions now requires distant_coast_chunk_seconds (default 1000 seconds).
No serde defaults repair older checkpoints: FleetCheckpoint12 and flight
model32/31 checkpoints or journals are explicitly incompatible. Model33 and
FleetCheckpoint13 preserve the existing world5 and Craft3 data formats.

Long segments require all vessels in Orbit and clear of rails blockers. A
closed half-distance domain bounds each body's and vessel's GM/J2 acceleration;
whole-interval displacement spheres guard all body/vessel and vessel/vessel
pairs. These include hull radii, terrain maximum height, sea level, atmosphere
ceiling and ground bands. Relative states use split frame-tree subtraction.
Short near-field policy and the existing ground-band/encounter checks remain.
No body or gravity source is omitted and no simulated time is skipped.

Limitations: sleeping ground scenes or any near-field craft keep the entire
Fleet on short chunks. The mathematical acceleration bound assumes the current
Newtonian model; EphemerisSource has no formal interpolation error-bound
interface for arbitrary custom sources. Existing conservative body-fixed
ground-band speed still applies and can additionally shorten a selected chunk.
Active propulsion on long warp and bounded-progress world-extension requests
remain separate work. Relativity is not implemented by this change.
